GCSD Culinary Pathway

Greece Culinary Work-Based Learning

From classroom kitchen to career.

Greece Culinary Work-Based Learning connects classroom instruction, professional kitchen practice, school-based enterprise, and employer partnerships. Students build culinary competence, professional habits, and authentic experience as they move from classroom kitchen to career.

Coordinated Sequence

A connected progression built on existing culinary practice

Students do not move through four isolated classes. Each stage adds responsibility, documentation, and readiness evidence for the next.

01

Culinary 1 & 2

Foundations, safety, sanitation, lab routines, knife skills, recipe execution, and professional kitchen habits.

Evidence: safety checks, lab performance, attendance, reflection.
02

Advanced Culinary

Scratch production, menu planning, costing, event preparation, certification preparation, and customer awareness.

Evidence: production records, cost/yield work, technical skill.
03

Kitchen Management

Leadership, station assignments, scheduling, enterprise records, event debriefs, and operational improvement.

Evidence: event analytics, team performance, leadership artifacts.
04

Senior Cooperative WBL

The next structured opportunity: supervised workplace learning, employer feedback, portfolio evidence, and exit presentation after approval.

Evidence: verified hours, employer evaluation, portfolio.
Every experience should create evidence. Evidence guides the next responsibility.

Culinary Passport and Gateway System: readiness is accumulated through observable evidence rather than assumed from interest alone.

Student-Run Culinary Enterprise and Event Analytics: catering, pop-ups, and school-based enterprise turn production into measurable learning.

Senior Cooperative Experience and Portfolio: external placement is positioned as a capstone extension, not a claim that placement is already operating.
