GCSD Culinary Pathway

Greece Culinary Work-Based Learning

Classroom instruction, professional kitchen practice, school-based enterprise, and employer partnerships.

Who it serves

Students, families, employers, district leaders, Board members, and pathway staff.

What students do

Safety, sanitation, lab production, menu planning, costing, catering, pop-ups, career exploration, portfolio evidence.

Pathway progression

Culinary 1 & 2 to Advanced Culinary to Kitchen Management to Senior Cooperative WBL.

What students gain

Technical skill, professional habits, certification preparation, employer feedback, and an exit portfolio.

How partners help

Guest visits, demonstrations, site visits, mock interviews, event feedback, and future approved training stations.

Success measures

Participation, WBL hours, certification, partners, Employability Profile growth, attendance, portfolio completion, employer feedback.
