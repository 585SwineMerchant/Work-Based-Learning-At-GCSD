For Families

Responsibility grows one demonstrated step at a time.

Students are not simply sent to a job. They build safety, skill, and professional habits in supervised settings before outside placement is considered.

How the pathway supports students

Prepare first. Document growth. Connect when ready.

01 · Prepare

Learn in a controlled kitchen

Safety, sanitation, equipment, recipes, teamwork, uniform expectations, and dependable work habits are taught and practiced.

02 · Demonstrate

Build a record of readiness

Lab work, events, attendance, Employability Profile feedback, gateways, and portfolio artifacts show what a student can do consistently.

03 · Connect

Add authentic responsibility

School-based enterprise and employer-connected experiences add real audiences, deadlines, feedback, and—after approval—supervised placement.

Questions families should be able to answer

Support is practical, not vague.

Final external-placement procedures require district approval, but the pathway’s support responsibilities are already clear.

How is readiness decided?

Through safety and technical performance, attendance, punctuality, communication, teamwork, reflection, and completed gateway evidence.

How is progress documented?

Through the Culinary Passport, Employability Profile, lab and event records, certification results, verified hours, feedback, and portfolio work.

Who supports the student?

The culinary teacher and pathway team; later, the WBL coordinator and approved employer supervisor join the communication and review process.

What must families plan for?

Professional expectations, reliable attendance, communication, transportation for approved experiences, and maintaining required documentation.
