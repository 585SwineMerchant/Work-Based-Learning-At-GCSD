Families

Support grows as responsibility grows

Families should know what exists now, what is being organized, and what requires approval before senior placement begins.

Current pathway foundation

Students already learn through labs, certification preparation, catering, pop-ups, guest professionals, restaurant exposure, menu planning, costing, and school-based enterprise.

Readiness before placement

Students show safety, sanitation, attendance, punctuality, professional appearance, communication, teamwork, technical skill, and reflection before higher-responsibility opportunities.

Family questions to plan for

  • Transportation expectations for approved external experiences.
  • How hours and feedback will be documented.
  • How school staff, employer supervisors, and families communicate.
  • What portfolio evidence students must maintain.

What still requires approval

Formal external senior cooperative placement, training-station approval, transportation procedures, and final forms must be determined through district approval before operation.

Greece Culinary Work-Based Learning

Greece Culinary WBL connects classroom instruction, professional kitchen practice, school-based enterprise, and employer-connected experiences.

Status

Existing culinary instruction, labs, certification preparation, catering, pop-ups, guest professionals, restaurant exposure, menu planning, costing, production, and school-based enterprise are current pathway strengths.
