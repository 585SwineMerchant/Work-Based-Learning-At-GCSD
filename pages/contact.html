Contact

Find the right pathway conversation

Different partners need different next steps. This page keeps the route clear without inventing a form or personal contact detail that has not been authorized.

Students and families

Start with the Culinary Pathway teacher or the school/district CTE contact for course questions, readiness expectations, family planning, and senior opportunity questions.

Guest professionals

Offer a demonstration, career talk, product critique, mock interview, or portfolio review connected to current classroom and enterprise work.

Site-visit hosts

Discuss a supervised visit that helps students connect classroom skill to workplace standards.

Event and community partners

Explore appropriate catering, pop-up, feedback, or community-facing learning opportunities through school channels.

Advisory participants

Support program alignment, workforce expectations, and review of evidence systems.

Future placement partners

Formal training-station participation must wait for district-approved senior cooperative WBL procedures.
