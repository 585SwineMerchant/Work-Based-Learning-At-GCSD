Outcomes

How success will be measured

The pathway should be accountable without inventing current performance data. Baselines and targets belong in the annual review cycle once implementation is authorized.

Student participationEnrollment and active participation by course and experience type.
WBL hoursDocumented school-based and employer-connected hours.
Industry certificationServSafe and YouScience Culinary 3 preparation and results when available.
Partner engagementGuest professionals, site visits, event partners, and approved training stations.
Employability Profile growthAttendance, punctuality, communication, teamwork, initiative, and professionalism.
Portfolio completionEvidence artifacts, reflections, employer feedback, and exit presentation completion.
