Outcomes & Measurement

Evidence should lead to a decision—not sit in a folder.

The pathway documents student growth and program performance so teachers can respond, students can advance, and the district can improve what it offers.

Student-level measurement

Three evidence streams tell a fuller story than a grade alone.

Each stream identifies what is recorded, when it is reviewed, and what decision it supports.

Culinary Passport

Readiness across six gateways

RecordsSafety, sanitation, lab performance, attendance, reflection, portfolio artifacts, and gateway completion.
ReviewedDuring labs, events, and formal readiness checkpoints.
GuidesWhat responsibility or experience a student is ready to attempt next.

Employability Profile

Professional habits over time

RecordsAttendance, punctuality, appearance, quality, resource management, communication, problem solving, ethics, teamwork, foundations, and safety.
ReviewedAt course checkpoints, enterprise experiences, and employer-connected experiences.
GuidesCoaching, added instruction, recognition, and workplace-readiness decisions.

Technical & Portfolio Evidence

Skill applied in context

RecordsEquipment, products, sanitation, techniques, culinary math, costing, menus, service, leadership, hours, feedback, and reflection.
ReviewedAcross labs, practical work, events, portfolio checkpoints, and approved workplace experiences.
GuidesTechnical priorities, reassessment, certification preparation, and final pathway evidence.
Program-level scorecard

The annual review asks four connected questions.

Baselines and targets are reported only when valid data exists; the system does not invent current results.

01Who is participating?

  • Enrollment by course
  • Experience type
  • WBL hours and distribution
  • Employer and community participation

02Are students becoming ready?

  • Gateway completion
  • Attendance and punctuality
  • Employability Profile growth
  • Technical-skill growth

03Are experiences producing value?

  • Certification results
  • Portfolio completion
  • Event execution
  • Client and employer feedback

04Where do students go next?

  • Exit presentation
  • Continued training
  • Postsecondary education
  • Employment where available
