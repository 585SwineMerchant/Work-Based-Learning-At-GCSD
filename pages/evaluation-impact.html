Outcomes and Measurement

Accountability through evidence, not guesswork

The pathway measures student progress and program performance by collecting evidence during labs, events, employer-connected experiences, gateway checks, portfolio reviews, and annual planning.

Program-level review

Baselines, targets, and current results should be reported only when they exist. The public accountability system should organize the following measures without inventing data.

Enrollment and participationGateway completionWBL hours and distributionCertification participation and resultsAttendance and punctuality patternsEmployability Profile growthTechnical-skill growthPortfolio completionEmployer/community participationEmployer evaluations and satisfactionEvent execution and client feedbackPostsecondary education, employment, or continued training where available

Event and enterprise analytics

Catering, pop-ups, and school-based enterprise should document more than participation. They can show planned versus actual food cost, recipe yield and portion accuracy, ingredient use, waste, production time, on-time completion, quality and consistency, sanitation observations, customer or client feedback, sales or output where applicable, team and leadership performance, post-event reflection, and corrective action.

Status

Existing culinary instruction, labs, certification preparation, catering, pop-ups, guest professionals, restaurant exposure, menu planning, costing, production, and school-based enterprise are current pathway strengths. Formal external senior cooperative WBL placement and training-station procedures require district approval before operation.
