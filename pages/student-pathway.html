Students

What you do, what you prove, what opens next

The pathway builds responsibility in stages. Your work in the kitchen becomes evidence of what you are ready to attempt next.

Culinary 1 & 2

Learn safe kitchen movement, sanitation, knife skills, mise en place, recipe reading, teamwork, and clean production habits.

You produce
Lab work, safety evidence, reflections, attendance and punctuality records.
Next opportunity
More complex production and event-connected work.

Advanced Culinary

Prepare food for real deadlines, plan menus, calculate cost, control yield, and connect production quality to customer expectations.

You produce
Production records, menu/costing artifacts, certification preparation evidence.
Next opportunity
Leadership, school-based enterprise, and readiness gateways.

Kitchen Management

Help assign roles, track timing, read event data, debrief results, and improve the next service.

You produce
Station plans, event analytics, leadership evidence, portfolio artifacts.
Next opportunity
Senior cooperative WBL readiness review after approval.

Senior Cooperative WBL

The senior extension connects prepared students to supervised workplace learning when district procedures are approved.

You produce
Verified hours, employer feedback, reflection, portfolio, exit presentation.
Next opportunity
Postsecondary education, employment, or continued training.

Greece Culinary Work-Based Learning

Greece Culinary WBL connects classroom instruction, professional kitchen practice, school-based enterprise, and employer-connected experiences.

Presentation Routes

  • Table display
  • Attention loop
  • Support screen

Status

Existing culinary instruction, labs, certification preparation, catering, pop-ups, guest professionals, restaurant exposure, menu planning, costing, production, and school-based enterprise are current pathway strengths. Formal external senior cooperative WBL placement and training-station procedures require district approval before operation.
