Students

What you will do in the Culinary Pathway

You start with classroom kitchen foundations, build real production habits, and move toward employer-connected experience when your readiness evidence is strong.

Progression

  • Culinary 1 & 2: safety, sanitation, knife skills, recipes, teamwork.
  • Advanced Culinary: production, menu planning, costing, certification preparation.
  • Kitchen Management: leadership, service systems, event analytics, school-based enterprise.
  • Senior Cooperative WBL: supervised workplace opportunity after district approval and readiness review.

Readiness Gateways

Readiness is shown through attendance, punctuality, safety, sanitation, technical skill, communication, professionalism, reflection, and portfolio evidence. The Culinary Passport helps make that progress visible.

Greece Culinary Work-Based Learning

Classroom instruction, professional kitchen practice, school-based enterprise, and employer-connected experiences.

Presentation Routes

  • Table display
  • Attention loop
  • Support screen

Status

External senior cooperative WBL and formal employer placement procedures require district approval before operation. Existing culinary instruction, labs, certification preparation, catering, pop-ups, and school-based enterprise are described as current pathway strengths.
