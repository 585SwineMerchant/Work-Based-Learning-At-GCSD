What readiness means

Readiness is demonstrated, not assumed.

Teachers look across technical performance and professional habits before students move into higher-responsibility work.

1

Safe and dependable: sanitation, equipment use, attendance, punctuality, and professional appearance.

2

Technically prepared: recipes, knife skills, production methods, quality, timing, and station organization.

3

Ready to work with others: communication, teamwork, problem solving, feedback, and reflection.
