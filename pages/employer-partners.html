Employers

Partner with prepared culinary students

Employer participation can begin small: guest speaking, demonstrations, mock interviews, site visits, menu feedback, event critique, or future supervised training-station placements.

Ways to participate

  • Visit class as a guest professional.
  • Host a site visit or career conversation.
  • Review portfolios, resumes, or mock interviews.
  • Support catering, pop-up, or menu feedback.
  • Consider a training-station role when formal senior WBL procedures are approved.

How students are prepared

Students build safety, sanitation, teamwork, knife skills, recipe execution, production habits, attendance, and employability evidence before outside placement is considered.

Greece Culinary Work-Based Learning

Classroom instruction, professional kitchen practice, school-based enterprise, and employer-connected experiences.

Presentation Routes

  • Table display
  • Attention loop
  • Support screen

Status

External senior cooperative WBL and formal employer placement procedures require district approval before operation. Existing culinary instruction, labs, certification preparation, catering, pop-ups, and school-based enterprise are described as current pathway strengths.
