Employer Partners

Start with a useful contribution. Grow when the fit is right.

Employer participation does not have to begin with a placement. The strongest partnerships often start with a modest, concrete role.

1

Guest professional

Share a career path, demonstrate a skill, critique a product, or explain workplace expectations.

2

Site visit or event partner

Host career exploration, support a pop-up or catering reflection, or provide menu and service feedback.

3

Portfolio and interview support

Review resumes, conduct mock interviews, comment on readiness evidence, or join advisory conversations.

4

Future training station

After approval, a prepared worksite may host supervised senior cooperative WBL with agreed expectations, feedback, and documentation.

What the school contributes

Student preparation, readiness review, communication, learning goals, documentation tools, and follow-up.

What the employer contributes

Realistic feedback, workplace context, skill demonstrations, professional expectations, and, when approved, supervised training-station experience.

Greece Culinary Work-Based Learning

Greece Culinary WBL connects classroom instruction, professional kitchen practice, school-based enterprise, and employer-connected experiences.

Presentation Routes

  • Table display
  • Attention loop
  • Support screen

Status

Existing culinary instruction, labs, certification preparation, catering, pop-ups, guest professionals, restaurant exposure, menu planning, costing, production, and school-based enterprise are current pathway strengths. Formal external senior cooperative WBL placement and training-station procedures require district approval before operation.
