The operating structure

The binder turns a placement into supervised learning.

It is the student’s field guide, individual learning plan, communication system, evidence portfolio, and accountability tool.

  • Guides expectations, safety, responsibilities, and professional practice
  • Connects the student, coordinator, and employer around the same goals
  • Documents hours, work, feedback, reflection, and employability growth
  • Supports check-ins, site visits, problem-solving, evaluation, and next steps
