School-Based Enterprise

The school kitchen becomes a workplace laboratory.

Catering, pop-ups, and student-run production add real clients, deadlines, standards, and decisions—while instruction and supervision remain close.

One event · four connected phases

Students plan the work, execute it, measure it, and improve it.

01 · Plan

Build the operation

Theme, client needs, menu, recipe scaling, cost estimate, schedule, stations, marketing or signage.

02 · Produce

Deliver to a standard

Mise en place, production, sanitation, quality control, packaging or service, and teamwork.

03 · Measure

Read what happened

Food cost, yield, waste, timing, on-time completion, product quality, and customer feedback.

04 · Reflect

Improve the next event

Debrief, corrective action, leadership notes, portfolio artifacts, and revised systems.

Two courses · one operation

Advanced Culinary produces. Kitchen Management reads the operation.

Advanced Culinary turns recipes, prep, costing, and service into authentic evidence. Kitchen Management uses the same evidence to teach scheduling, supervision, financial decisions, and continuous improvement.
