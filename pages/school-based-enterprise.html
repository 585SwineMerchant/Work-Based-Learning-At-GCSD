School-Based Enterprise

The school kitchen is the first workplace laboratory

Catering, pop-ups, and student-run production create real deadlines, real standards, and real data before students move toward outside placement.

Plan

Theme, menu, recipe scaling, cost estimate, schedule, station assignments, marketing or signage.

Produce

Mise en place, production, packaging or service, sanitation, quality control, teamwork.

Measure

Food cost, yield, ingredient use, waste, timing, on-time completion, customer or client feedback.

Reflect

Debrief, corrective action, leadership notes, portfolio artifact, next-event improvement.

Advanced Culinary produces. Kitchen Management reads the operation.

Advanced Culinary students create authentic production evidence through planning, prep, costing, and service. Kitchen Management uses those records to teach scheduling, supervision, financial decision-making, event analysis, and continuous improvement.
