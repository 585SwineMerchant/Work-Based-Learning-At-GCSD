Work-Based Learning

A structured path from classroom kitchen to career

Culinary WBL connects instruction, kitchen labs, school-based enterprise, and employer-connected experience so students can show both technical skill and professional readiness.

It is not just a job

Students build evidence through safety, sanitation, lab production, attendance, punctuality, teamwork, communication, reflection, and portfolio artifacts before they move into higher-responsibility experiences.

It builds on what already exists

The Culinary Pathway already includes certification preparation, kitchen labs, catering, pop-ups, guest professionals, restaurant exposure, menu planning, costing, and school-based enterprise.

It adds a senior opportunity

The senior cooperative WBL experience is the next structured extension: supervised placement, employer evaluation, professional portfolio, and an exit presentation after district approval.

It gives partners a clear role

Employers and community partners can support students through guest visits, demonstrations, site visits, mock interviews, feedback, advisory participation, and future approved training-station placements.
