Work-Based Learning

A structured path, not just a job

Culinary WBL connects classroom learning, supervised kitchen practice, school-based enterprise, and employer-connected experiences so students can show both technical skill and professional readiness.

Built from the work already happening

The Culinary Pathway already includes kitchen laboratories, certification preparation, guest professionals, restaurant exposure, catering, pop-ups, menu planning, costing, production, and school-based enterprise. WBL gives that work a clearer evidence system.

  • Career awareness becomes career preparation.
  • Lab habits become documented readiness.
  • School-based enterprise becomes a bridge to employer-connected learning.
  • Senior cooperative WBL becomes the next approved extension.
Experience

Kitchen lab, event, guest professional, site visit, enterprise, placement.

Evidence

Passport gateway, Employability Profile, technical skill, hours, reflection, feedback.

Decision

Next responsibility, added instruction, leadership role, placement readiness, program improvement.
