Work-Based Learning

A structured path, not just a job

Culinary WBL connects classroom learning, authentic production, school-based enterprise, and supervised employer experience—then makes student growth visible.

The proposal connects the pieces

Existing strength becomes a documented progression.

Work-based learning does not replace the Culinary Pathway. It gives the work students already do a deliberate sequence, common readiness evidence, and a supervised senior extension.

01 · Current foundation

Learn and practice

Technical instruction, kitchen labs, certification, events, and school-based enterprise build skill and professional habits.

02 · WBL structure

Prove readiness

Gateways, an Employability Profile, work samples, reflection, feedback, and portfolio evidence show what students can do consistently.

03 · Proposed extension

Apply with support

A senior cooperative course adds an approved placement, individual learning goals, verified hours, coordinator contact, employer evaluation, and a capstone portfolio.

The essential difference

Employment is activity. WBL is learning by design.

IntentionalWork is connected to pathway standards and individual goals.
SupervisedStudent, school, and employer share expectations and communication.
DocumentedHours, evidence, feedback, and reflection are reviewed—not merely collected.
ProgressiveEvidence determines the next responsibility, support, or readiness decision.

Status
